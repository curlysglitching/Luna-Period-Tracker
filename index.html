Welcome to Luna ☽
Your personal period & cycle tracker — here’s how everything works.
📅 Logging Your Period
‹›Use the arrows beside the month name to flip between months, and the year arrows at the very top to jump whole years.
👆Tap any day to open the day menu.
💧Choose Light, Medium, or Heavy flow. The square fills with your chosen color.
🎨Change your period color anytime from the swatches at the top.
🌸 Symptoms & Notes
🏷Tap any symptom tag — cramps, bloating, fatigue, mood swings, and more.
🤯Log Headache or Migraine separately. A small icon appears on the calendar day so you can see patterns at a glance.
📝Use the note field for anything extra — medications, observations, etc.
🔮 Smart Predictions
🟠Predicted period days show a dashed orange border.
!A purple ! marks each day of your fertile window.
🌸Your predicted ovulation day gets a purple border.
📊 Cycle & Headache Stats
🔄The first stats row tracks your cycle length, period length, and next predicted dates.
⚡The second stats row tracks your headache and migraine frequency — monthly averages, yearly total, and your worst month.
🔒 Privacy
🔒Everything saves to your browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere — completely private on your device.

💾 Backup & Restore
📥Because your data lives only in this browser, clearing browser data would erase it. Export a backup file to keep it safe or move it to another device, then Import it there.
